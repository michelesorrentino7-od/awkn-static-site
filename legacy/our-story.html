MEET THE SISTERS BEHIND AWKN

“

During my career in tech and start-ups, I constantly navigated the challenge of balancing high-performance workdays with vibrant social nights.

That experience sparked my passion to share Korea’s time-honoured recovery rituals, especially those traditionally used before and after moments of celebration with a wider audience.

”
Tess
Co-Founder
“

For years, I found myself holding back at social gatherings with alcohol, due to a common sensitivity experienced by many people of East Asian descent often referred to as “Asian Flush.” The visible effects made me feel uncomfortable and self-conscious.

With AWKN, I’ve found a personal ritual that lets me immerse myself in life’s celebrations with newfound confidence and joy.

”
Soom
Co-Founder
