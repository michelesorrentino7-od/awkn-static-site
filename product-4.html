FREQUENTLY ASKED

When is the best time to take AWKN?

We recommend taking one shot towards the start of your evening. If you have extended plans than usual, we recommend taking additional shots. As AWKN works while you sleep it’s important that you take AWKN before going to bed.

Is AWKN a medicine?

No — AWKN is not a pharmaceutical product. It is classified as a food product and should be used as part of a balanced lifestyle. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or under medical supervision, please consult a healthcare professional before use.

AWKN is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. This product will not prevent intoxication or promote sobriety. Please consume responsibly.

Is AWKN Food Safety Approved?

AWKN is manufactured in South Korea by one of the country's leading OEM producers, operating under internationally recognised food safety standards. The facility holds certifications including U.S. FDA-registered, MFDS-compliant (Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety), FSSC 22000, KIWA, and HACCP. Our formulation also meets UK and EU food supplement regulations.

What happens if I forget to take AWKN at the beginning of the evening

No worries — As AWKN works while you sleep it’s important that you take AWKN before going to bed. For extra efficacy, we recommend taking two or more shots if you have extended plans than usual. AWKN is designed to complement your lifestyle, so feel free to take one or two shots depending on your evening plans.

What does AWKN taste like?

AWKN is a naturally flavoured herbal blend inspired by traditional Korean wellness practices. It features a bold pear taste with subtle fruity notes and a refreshing finish.

Does AWKN contain any allergens?

AWKN does not contain any intentionally added allergens. However, it is manufactured in a facility that also handles egg, peanuts, other nuts, cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, molluscs, and fish. As a result, traces of these allergens may be present.

What are the ingredients?

Distilled Water, Pear, Choline, Red Ginseng, Ginger, Turmeric, Reishi Mushroom, Glutathione, Taurine, Vitamin C, Live Cultures(Probiotic), Vitamin B1&B6, Artichoke, Betaine, Selenium
